Crisis Support Resources and First Response Protocol (India)

If you or someone you know is in immediate crisis, having suicidal thoughts, or plans of self-harm, please refer to the following national helplines:

1. Government and Public Helplines:
   - Kiran Mental Health Helpline: 1800-599-0019 (24/7, Toll-free, Multilingual)
   - Tele-MANAS (Government of India): 14416 or 1800-891-4416 (24/7, Toll-free, available in multiple regional languages)

2. NGO and Non-Profit Helplines:
   - AASRA Helpline: +91-9820466726 (24/7, English & Hindi support)
   - Vandrevala Foundation: +91-9999666555 (24/7, Multilingual counseling)
   - iCall (TISS): +91-9152987821 (Monday to Saturday, 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM)
   - Sneha India: +91-44-24640050 (24/7, English, Tamil)

First Response Protocol for Self-Harm/Suicidal Ideation:
- DO NOT ignore warning signs or comments about suicide.
- DO NOT lecture, argue, or try to convince them that life is worth living. Listen without judgment.
- Keep them safe: Reduce access to potential means of self-harm (pills, sharp objects, access to heights).
- Direct them to professional help: Facilitate connection with a helpline or guide them to the nearest hospital psychiatric department.
- Follow up: Stay in touch with the person to show that they are cared for and supported.
