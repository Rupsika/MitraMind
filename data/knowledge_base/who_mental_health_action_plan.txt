WHO Mental Health Action Plan 2013-2030

Goal 1: Promote mental well-being, prevent mental disorders, and reduce mortality from mental health conditions.

Key Principles:
1. Universal Health Coverage: All individuals should have access to essential mental health services without facing financial hardship.
2. Human Rights: Mental health policies and laws should comply with the Convention on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities.
3. Life-course Approach: Interventions must address the needs of individuals at different stages of life, including infancy, childhood, adolescence, adulthood, and older age.
4. Multisectoral Approach: Action is needed across health, education, employment, justice, and social sectors.
5. Empowerment: Persons with mental disorders should be empowered and involved in mental health advocacy and policy design.

Recommended Actions for Well-being & Stress Management:
- Promote physical activity: Regular exercise is linked to improved mood, lower anxiety levels, and better cognitive function.
- Encourage healthy sleep hygiene: Maintaining a consistent sleep schedule, limiting screen time before bed, and creating a comfortable sleep environment.
- Encourage social connection: Strong social networks protect against depression and support recovery.
- Promote mindfulness and relaxation techniques: Practices like breathing exercises, progressive muscle relaxation, and meditation reduce physiological stress markers.
- Reduce access to means of self-harm and suicide: Implementing restrictions on common lethal means.

Mental Health in the Workplace:
- Workplaces should create supportive environments that promote work-life balance.
- Address organizational risk factors such as heavy workloads, lack of control, and workplace bullying.
- Implement training programs for managers to recognize signs of distress in employees and support them effectively.
